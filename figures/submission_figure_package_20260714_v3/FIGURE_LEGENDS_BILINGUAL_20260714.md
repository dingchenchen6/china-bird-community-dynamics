# 投稿主图双语图注

## Fig. 1

**Fig. 1 | Evidence architecture and inference boundaries for Chinese bird community change.** **a**, Occurrence records are aggregated to 1,247 100-km grid cells and five periods spanning 2000-2024. The 200-species `stMsPGOcc` model includes spatial NNGP and temporal AR(1) structure and supports the primary spatial inference; the 500-species `tMsPGOcc` model is non-spatial and provides temporal breadth support. **b**, Model-to-claim matrix distinguishing primary, breadth, descriptive and complete-case evidence. The 200-species four-chain diagnostic table has a maximum R-hat of 1.039 and minimum ESS of 70; R-hat is unavailable in the current 500-species diagnostic table. Source data are provided as a Source Data file.

**图1 | 中国鸟类群落变化的证据架构与推断边界。** **a**，将鸟类出现记录汇总到1,247个100 km网格和2000-2024年的五个时期。包含空间NNGP与时间AR(1)结构的200物种`stMsPGOcc`模型承担主要空间推断；非空间500物种`tMsPGOcc`模型提供时间和物种池广度支持。**b**，模型与结论之间的证据等级矩阵，区分主要证据、广度支持、描述性结果和完整案例分析。200物种四链诊断表的最大R-hat为1.039、最小ESS为70；当前500物种诊断表缺少R-hat。Source Data见对应文件。

## Fig. 2

**Fig. 2 | Spatial multidiversity across five survey periods in the primary spatial model.** Occupancy-corrected richness, Shannon diversity, functional volume, phylogenetic diversity, mean phylogenetic distance and Rao's Q are shown for 2000-2004 through 2020-2024. Values are rescaled from low to high within each metric using a scale fixed across periods; colours therefore support temporal and spatial comparison within a row but not numerical comparison among metrics. Grey cells lack an estimate. Estimates derive from the 200-species spatial model over 1,247 100-km grid cells. Source data are provided as a Source Data file.

**图2 | 空间主模型中五个调查时期的多维多样性格局。** 展示2000-2004至2020-2024年的检测校正丰富度、Shannon多样性、功能体积、系统发育多样性、平均系统发育距离和Rao's Q。每个指标使用跨时期固定的指标内相对色标，因此颜色可用于同一行的时空比较，但不能在不同指标之间作数值比较。灰色网格表示无估计值。结果来自1,247个100 km网格的200物种空间模型。Source Data见对应文件。

## Fig. 3

**Fig. 3 | Spatial fingerprints of multidimensional community change.** Standardized grid-cell trends are shown for six diversity dimensions from the 200-species spatial model. Blue denotes relative decreases and orange relative increases after scaling each metric by its maximum absolute trend. Fully saturated cells have 95% posterior credible intervals excluding zero; pale cells have intervals overlapping zero. The map supports comparison of spatial direction and relative strength within each metric, not comparison of raw slope units among metrics. Source data are provided as a Source Data file.

**图3 | 多维度群落变化的空间指纹。** 展示200物种空间模型中六个多样性维度的网格标准化趋势。蓝色表示相对下降，橙色表示相对上升；每个指标按其最大绝对趋势进行标准化。高饱和度网格的95%后验可信区间不跨零，浅色网格的区间跨零。该图支持比较同一指标内部的空间方向和相对强度，不支持不同指标原始斜率单位之间的直接比较。Source Data见对应文件。

## Fig. 4

**Fig. 4 | Decoupling of taxonomic, phylogenetic and functional change.** **a**, Change from 2000-2004 to 2020-2024 expressed in baseline spatial standard-deviation units for the 200-species spatial model and 500-species temporal model. Lines connect estimates for the same metric; circle and square symbols distinguish model scope. **b**, National trajectories for eight core metrics in the 500-species temporal breadth extension. Lines show across-grid mean values relative to the 2000-2004 baseline; ribbons show the corresponding mean of grid-level 95% posterior intervals and should not be interpreted as a national posterior credible interval. Taxonomic metrics are blue, phylogenetic metrics purple and functional metrics orange. Source data are provided as a Source Data file.

**图4 | 分类、系统发育与功能变化的解耦。** **a**，比较200物种空间模型和500物种时间模型从2000-2004到2020-2024的端点变化，效应量以基线空间标准差表示；连线连接同一指标，圆形和方形区分模型范围。**b**，500物种时间广度扩展中八个核心指标的全国轨迹。曲线为跨网格均值相对基线的变化，色带为网格95%后验区间的均值，不能解释为严格的全国后验可信区间。分类指标为蓝色、系统发育指标为紫色、功能指标为橙色。Source Data见对应文件。

## Fig. 5

**Fig. 5 | Late turnover collapse and the identity of species winners.** **a**, Turnover share of probability-weighted temporal beta diversity across adjacent five-year periods in the 200-species spatial and 500-species temporal models. Points are posterior means and error bars are 95% credible intervals across 1,247 grid cells; the dashed line marks equal turnover and nestedness shares. **b**, Numbers and proportions of increasing, stable and declining species in the 500-species model. Species are increasing when the posterior probability of a positive trend exceeds 0.95, declining when the probability of a negative trend exceeds 0.95, and stable otherwise. **c**, Six strongest credible increases and decreases; points and error bars show posterior trend means and 95% credible intervals. **d**, Spearman associations between species trends and diet specialization or habitat breadth in the complete-case subset (n=200 species); points are posterior means and intervals are 95% posterior intervals. Source data are provided as a Source Data file.

**图5 | 晚期周转崩塌与扩张物种的生态身份。** **a**，200物种空间模型和500物种时间模型中，相邻五年时期概率加权时间beta多样性的turnover比例。点为后验均值，误差线为基于1,247个网格的95%可信区间；虚线表示turnover与nestedness各占50%。**b**，500物种模型中扩张、稳定和收缩物种的数量与比例。正趋势后验概率大于0.95定义为扩张，负趋势后验概率大于0.95定义为收缩，其余为稳定。**c**，六个最强可信扩张和收缩物种；点和误差线分别表示后验趋势均值及95%可信区间。**d**，完整案例子集（n=200物种）中物种趋势与食性专化或栖息地广度的Spearman相关；点为后验均值，区间为95%后验区间。Source Data见对应文件。

## Fig. 6

**Fig. 6 | Detection correction changes local inference while spatial context dominates measured drivers.** **a**, Naive observed-richness trends versus occupancy-corrected richness trends for 1,247 grid cells in the 500-species model. Orange points identify 25 grid cells in which trend direction changes; the solid line is the least-squares fit and the dashed line is the 1:1 relationship. **b**, Distribution of corrected-minus-naive trend differences. The orange line marks the mean difference of -12.0 species per period and the dashed line marks zero. **c**, Random-forest permutation importance for four predictor groups across 100 posterior draws; points are means and error bars are empirical 95% intervals. **d**, Pure adjusted R-squared fractions from variation partitioning of the corrected-richness trend. Driver panels represent spatially structured associations and predictive importance, not causal effects. Source data are provided as a Source Data file.

**图6 | 检测校正改变局地推断，而空间背景主导已测驱动。** **a**，500物种模型中1,247个网格的朴素观测丰富度趋势与占域校正丰富度趋势。橙色点表示趋势方向发生改变的25个网格；实线为最小二乘拟合，虚线为1:1关系。**b**，校正趋势减去朴素趋势的分布。橙线表示每时期-12.0个物种的平均差异，虚线表示零。**c**，100次后验抽样中四类预测变量的随机森林置换重要性；点为均值，误差线为经验95%区间。**d**，校正丰富度趋势的变异分解纯adjusted R-squared分量。驱动面板表示空间结构化关联和预测重要性，不支持因果解释。Source Data见对应文件。
